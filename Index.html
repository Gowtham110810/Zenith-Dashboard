  • The ₹2,70,000 (6-Month) Surplus Reserve: Because Section 44ADA effective tax rates are often much lower than 25%, a surplus accumulates. Build this surplus up to **₹2,70,000** as an emergency shield.

Pillar 1

0% GST Export

Foreign tech remittances qualify as Zero-Rated Exports. File an annual digital LUT on the GST portal to pay 0% GST without locking working capital.

Pillar 2

Section 44ADA

Declare 50% of your gross turnover as direct business expenses without bookkeeping or audits. Pay slab tax strictly on the remaining 50%.

Pillar 3

Advance Tax

Pay quarterly estimated taxes (15% June, 45% Sept, 75% Dec, 100% March) to completely avoid Section 234B/C penal interest charges.

Pillar 4

12.5% LTCG

Mutual funds held over 12 months qualify for 12.5% Long-Term Capital Gains. The first ₹1.25 Lakhs of profit per financial year is 100% exempt.
